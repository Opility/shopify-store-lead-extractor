# ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Q: How does tech stack detection work?
A: The engine scans script tags, DOM attributes, and network assets for known fingerprint signatures of popular apps like Klaviyo, Yotpo, Gorgias, and Recharge.

### Q: How is product catalog count calculated?
A: The scraper queries `/products.json?limit=250` endpoint on target store domains to return accurate product counts.

### Q: How much does it cost to run?
A: Pricing is Pay-Per-Result at **$2.00 per 1,000 extracted leads**.
